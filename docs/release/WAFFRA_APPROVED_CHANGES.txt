CURRENT USER-FACING CAPABILITIES (supported by existing Flutter/QA source contracts):
- Saudi edition: ledger currency is SAR; no country/currency-choice onboarding. Tool-based currency conversion is independent of stored SAR finance records. Gold and oil market views remain tool features.
- Home dashboard: encrypted verified account and transaction summary, account-specific balances and filters, budget/goal/recurring status, balance privacy eye, responsive cards and Arabic/English presentation.
- Manual finance: explicit income and expense entry, required verified financial account, optional budget association, searchable categories and linked parent/child accounts, two-sided transfer between own accounts (excluded from budget expenses).
- Accounts: distinguish local banks, digital banks and digital wallets; choose provider plus actual child money account. First-time account creation and historic account linking require explicit confirmation; do not silently invent money or duplicate SMS imports.
- Savings goals: independent goal-savings wallet with a selected verified bank/wallet funding account. Confirmed funding credits the wallet and debits the source exactly once via trusted SQLCipher; optional linked due and recurring default account keep their identities and prevent repeat posting.
- SMS: optional permission, locally reviewed bank SMS with sender/source verification; pending, approved and excluded messages have explicit user actions. No SMS is automatically recorded as a financial transaction without a verified, user-confirmed write. Original/OTP and anti-replay controls remain.
- Receipts: user-selected photos/attachments and encrypted storage/viewer are still supported; importing or recognizing financial transactions from photographed receipts is NOT a current feature.
- Current interface: شارة المرفقات shows the verified attachment count on applicable transactions; الملاحظات remain separately editable from protected original bank SMS; زر الإضافة opens the manual income/expense/transfer actions with a blurred background; الوضع الفاتح and the system-matched dark theme retain their Arabic/English layouts.
- Settings, authentication and data safety: phone credential/OS biometric owner verification, secure session/logout, encrypted backup/restore, SQLCipher-ledger source and legacy data retention safeguards.
- Tools: the maintained four practical tools (financial health, transaction search, monthly closing, safe diagnostics) and existing currency/gold/oil read-only market utilities. [M222] أسعار الذهب remains a real available gold-pricing/calculator feature, subject to verified live quote availability.

NOT CURRENT FEATURES:
- Old on-device Qwen model, automatic AI feature pane/installer and the removed on-device receipt OCR/Tesseract pipeline are NOT available in the Saudi UI.
- Previously removed bank-message paste/quick-capture buttons and obsolete AI templates are NOT offered as current Home actions. The separate SMS review path stays.
- Legacy Global-edition country/currency selection and foreign-SMS FX posting remain disabled in this Saudi release.
